# ⚖️ Juris-AI

## AI-Powered Multilingual Legal Accessibility Platform

Juris-AI is an AI-powered legal assistance platform designed to make legal information more accessible, understandable, and citizen-friendly across India.

The platform combines Artificial Intelligence, OCR (Optical Character Recognition), multilingual support, legal document analysis, legal section search, and legal drafting tools to help users better understand legal information.

- Clause identification

- Citizen-friendly summaries
- Download extracted text and analysis

- Ask legal questions in natural language

Search and understand legal sections from:

- Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)
